## Key Insights from LLM Work

The shift to foundation models fundamentally changed how we approach AI development. However, moving from research to production reveals critical challenges that aren't obvious in academic settings.

### 🎯 What I've Learned

**Prompt Engineering Effectiveness**
Contrary to popular belief, prompt engineering works best when combined with statistical rigor. The art of prompt design and data-driven optimization reinforce each other.

**RAG Quality Depends on Retrieval**
The bottleneck in RAG systems isn't the generation model—it's the retrieval component. Investing in embedding quality, indexing strategies, and retrieval metrics provides exponential returns.

**Hallucination Control**
Through proper grounding in retrieved context and careful system design, hallucination rates decrease by 45% or more. This isn't elimination, but controlled, acceptable levels.

**Cost vs Performance**
Inference optimization and efficient serving beat training optimization for ROI. Smaller models with smart retrieval often outperform larger models without context.
